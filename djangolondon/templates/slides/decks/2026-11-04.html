🪦 PEP 836 withdrawn

October 19, 2026

  • The PEP for CPython’s experimental JIT compiler
  • Steering Council required an accepted PEP to keep it in main
  • JIT will be removed early in the 3.16 cycle
  • Still in 3.15 as an experiment, off by default

peps.python.org/pep-0836
